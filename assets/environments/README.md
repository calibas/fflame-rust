# HDRI skies

Photographed skies all round that light a solid and are what its
reflections show (`escape::environment`; docs/main/TERRAIN.md, *HDRI
skies*). Each is listed in `ENVIRONMENTS` in `src/escape/environment.rs`,
with its display level, its median and its credit; a test checks the
median against the file.

| File | Sky | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sunflowers_puresky_1k.hdr` | Sunflowers (Pure Sky) | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/sunflowers_puresky) | CC0 |
| `rogland_clear_night_1k.hdr` | Rogland Clear Night | [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rogland_clear_night) | CC0 |

Both are the 1K (1024x512) Radiance `.hdr` downloads. Poly Haven asks,
not requires, a credit link: the Lighting & Material panel shows one under
the chosen sky.
